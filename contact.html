Common Questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery within Ghana is 3–5 business days. International orders take 7–14 business days.

Can I return a product?

Yes. We offer a 30-day return policy on all items in original condition. Contact us to start a return.

How do I become an artisan partner?

Select "Becoming an artisan partner" in the form and tell us about your craft. Our team will reach out within 3 business days.

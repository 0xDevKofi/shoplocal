Our Story

Connecting Artisans
with the World

ShopLocal was born in Accra with a simple belief: the incredible craft traditions of Ghana deserve a global stage — and the people who make them deserve a fair income.

Our Mission

We partner directly with skilled artisans across Ghana — weavers, potters, jewellers, woodcarvers — giving them a direct sales channel free from middlemen. Every purchase you make goes predominantly back to the maker.

We handle the logistics, the photography, and the platform, so artisans can focus on what they do best: creating beautiful, one-of-a-kind pieces that carry generations of knowledge and cultural meaning.

What We Stand For

🤝

Fair Trade

Artisans set their own prices. We take a small platform fee and never undercut their work. Transparency is baked into every transaction.

🌍

Sustainability

We prioritise artisans who use locally sourced, natural, or recycled materials. Our packaging is 100% recyclable.

🎨

Cultural Preservation

Every product on ShopLocal carries a story. We document techniques, origins, and the artisan behind each piece so that culture travels with the craft.

📦

Reliable Delivery

We work with trusted logistics partners across Ghana and internationally so your order arrives safely and on time.
